# Keep it in one AI, use it in another

These are copyable examples, not recorded demonstrations or promises about a particular client's live compatibility. Use two clients with verified access to the required tools.

## Your first cross-assistant result

1. In NugIt, create a manual collection called **Reusable context**.
2. Connect assistant A to save to NugIt. Ask it to write a short voice guide for an imaginary stationery shop: warm, practical, no hype.
3. Say **“Save that to NugIt with the title Stationery brand voice.”** Open the confirmed link.
4. In NugIt, add that Nug to **Reusable context**.
5. Connect assistant B to the same NugIt account. Request read permission and approve only **Reusable context**.
6. Ask: **“Search NugIt for stationery brand voice. Read the matching Nug, then draft a short launch announcement using that guidance. Cite the Nug you used.”**
7. Check the cited Nug and the draft. If you want to keep the new draft too, explicitly ask to save it and ensure assistant B also has write permission.

Success is a useful result in the second assistant, grounded in the actual saved Nug. A connection-success screen alone is not the outcome.

## Other useful starting points

| Work | Save | Reuse prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teaching | A lesson outline titled Fractions workshop | “Search NugIt for fractions workshop, read it, and adapt it for beginners. Cite the original.” |
| Writing | An editing checklist titled Editorial checklist | “Find and read my editorial checklist in NugIt. Apply it to this draft and explain the changes.” |
| Research | A synthesis titled Customer interview themes, with public citations | “Read the Nug matching customer interview themes. Separate the saved evidence from your new suggestions.” |
| Business | A decision record titled Pricing experiment | “Find pricing experiment in NugIt. Read it and use the stated constraints to assess these options.” |
| Development | A fix titled Deployment cache fix | “Find deployment cache fix in NugIt. Read it and assess whether its approach applies here before changing code.” |

Use descriptive titles and a small collection of material you actually want to reuse. Keyword search works best when your prompt includes words from the saved material. NugIt preserves selected text and links; it does not automatically transfer native project files, whole chat histories, or every AI's interactive artifact format.
